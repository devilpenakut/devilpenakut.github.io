# devilpenakut

Personal blog in Bahasa Indonesia, 2008–2023, 101 posts. Gadgets and iPhone, 2008-era Linux and modem how-tos, game consoles, product reviews, opinion pieces. Jekyll on GitHub Pages; no build step beyond Pages' own.

## Audience
Indonesian readers arriving mostly from search on a single old post, plus a few who know the author and browse. They want to read one article comfortably, then maybe find another.

## Job
Read a post. Find another post by year. Learn in one line what the blog is.

## Constraints
- Content is untouched: post files, URLs (`/:title/`), feed, sitemap stay as they are.
- 11 recent posts are title-only stubs from an import; the design must handle an empty body honestly.
- Images are hotlinked (wp.com, cloudinary); some posts carry legacy embed scripts.
- GitHub Pages' Jekyll 3 + Ruby Sass: keep SCSS plain.

## Brand commitments
- Visual world pinned by the owner: "Miranda" broadsheet (`../DESIGN MIRANDA.md`) — parchment, ink black, oversized compressed serif display, one ember-orange stamp accent.
- No invented facts about the author.
