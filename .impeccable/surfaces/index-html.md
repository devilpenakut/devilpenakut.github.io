Scope: whole blog (home/archive, post, page, 404). Mode: Read.
Audience: Indonesian readers landing on one old post from search; a few browsers.
Job: read comfortably, then find another post by year.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The blog as a newspaper morgue — every one of 101 posts filed under giant year numerals in a left drawer column, newest edition on top. Refuses the stock blog card-feed with "Read More" buttons.
OWN-WORLD: Miranda broadsheet (owner-pinned). Parchment #e2dedb ground, ink #1d1d1b, paper #ebe8e5 insets (lighter than bone-cream so the ember stamp clears 4.5:1), one ember stamp. Bodoni Moda black display crushed to lh .78 and negative tracking; Source Serif 4 light body; Pirata One blackletter nameplate only. Hairline ink rules, 0 radius images, no shadows except none.
STORY: Visitor sees a nameplate and what the paper is (101 tulisan sejak 2008), reads the lead story, scans years like a morgue drawer, opens a post that reads like a column.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Hairline header bar (Sejak 2008 · nameplate · Arsip/Tentang). Full-bleed ink banner, "DEVIL PENAKUT" in parchment Bodoni at ~17vw. Below: lead story — title in Bodoni 4–5rem left, drop-cap excerpt right, ember stamp seal "SEJAK 2008".
FORM: Morgue archive, position 7 on own list, seed b9b1170f. Raise from cutting-bench challenger: state is a mark, not a hue — title-only stubs carry a stamped "Judul saja" mark (plain Indonesian beats the cryptic "ARSIP") instead of a gray fade.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
